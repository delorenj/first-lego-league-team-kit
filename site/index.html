ONE DAD. EIGHT SPOTS. YOUR TOWN NEXT.

I'm a kindergarten dad who needs eight kids for my town's first FIRST® LEGO® League team — so I built the whole recruiting kit: a signup site, a print flyer, the outreach emails, even a backend that keeps signups on your own machine. It's live and recruiting Clinton kids right now — and it's yours too, free. One config file, one afternoon, no monthly fee.

  4. 4

    Step 4. Now it's yours. Pick your baseplate.

    Edit one file with your town's details, pick how to host it, done. The installer walks every path — you mostly press Enter.

    Email me the signups via a form service

    Formspree or a Google Sheet. No server, free, live in an hour.

    Docker Compose + built-in backend Your data, your box

    A Pi, a NAS, an old laptop. A SQLite file on your box, a private roster page, no third party reading your neighbors' emails.

    DigitalOcean Your data, your droplet

    A one-click droplet that owns its data — or free App Platform static hosting. The installer drives doctl.

    Your own reverse proxy Your data, your box

    Already run Traefik and a tunnel? The kit slots right in, fully parameterized.

    Read before building (please keep these)

    Privacy. The form asks for a child's first name and grade, plus a parent's contact — that's it. No last names, no birthdays, no photos. The minimum to reach a family. Delete it all at season's end. That's the whole privacy policy.

    Trademarks. FIRST® and LEGO® aren't ours. Every page ships the “not sponsored by or affiliated” disclaimer and the ® marks. Please leave them in.

  5. 5

    Step 5. Your turn.

    git clone https://github.com/delorenj/first-lego-league-team-kit.git
cd first-lego-league-team-kit
./install.sh   # a few plain questions → your site is live

    Not a techie? That's the whole point. The installer asks about you — your town, your school, your email — and you press Enter a lot. Or ask any AI assistant to run the deploy-team-site skill and it'll do it with you.
