# Social post — Facebook groups / Nextdoor / class text chains

Short, shareable copy to get your signup link in front of local K–2 families. Three lengths — use the
one that fits the channel. Pair with a bright photo (a LEGO/robot image or your flyer) for far more reach.

**Fill in:** `{{town}}`, `{{signupUrl}}`, `{{coachEmail}}`, `{{costPerChild}}`, `{{spots}}`.

**Where to post:** your school's PTO/HSA Facebook page (ask an officer to share it), local town / "moms of"
Facebook groups, and Nextdoor. Many groups require admin approval or ban "promotions" — frame it as a
**parent-led volunteer activity**, mention you're a local school parent, and it usually sails through.

---
