# Email — School / HSA pitch

Send to your school's HSA/PTA or principal to get the flyer in front of families. It asks for three
small, separable things so no single objection (usually budget) can sink the whole request.

**Fill in:** `{{coachName}}`, `{{schoolName}}`, `{{town}}`, `{{signupUrl}}`, `{{coachEmail}}`,
`{{backToSchoolNight}}` (date, if known).

---

**Subject:** Proposal: a FIRST LEGO League team for {{schoolName}} K–2 families

Hi {{hsaContact}},

I'm {{coachName}}, a {{schoolName}} parent. I'm starting {{town}}'s first **FIRST® LEGO® League team
for K–2 kids** this fall — the new K–2 program where 5–7 year-olds build and code motorized LEGO
models and finish the season at a friendly regional expo. It's a parent-led community team (I'll be a
volunteer coach), not a school-run program, but it's *for* our school's families, and I'd love the
HSA's help getting the word out.

Three things would make a big difference — any one of them helps, and none requires a budget commitment:

1. **A flyer slot at back-to-school night** ({{backToSchoolNight}}) so families can scan a QR code and sign up in 30 seconds.
2. **A weekly meeting space** (a classroom or the library, ~1 hour after school, Sept–winter) if one's available. If not, we'll host it off-site.
3. **Optional:** if the HSA ever funds enrichment, a small contribution toward kits would let us keep per-child cost down — but this is a nice-to-have, not a requirement. The team runs either way.

Roster is capped at 8, first come first served. Sign-up page: {{signupUrl}}

Happy to answer questions or present for two minutes at a meeting. Thanks for considering it!

{{coachName}}
{{coachEmail}}

*Organized by volunteer parents; not sponsored by or affiliated with FIRST, the LEGO Group, or {{schoolName}}.*
