# Code of Conduct

This is a project about helping kids. Let's act like it.

## The short version

**Be kind. Assume good faith. Remember there's a person — often a tired, well-meaning
volunteer parent — on the other end.**

## What we're going for

- **Welcoming.** Beginners, non-coders, first-time contributors, and people whose
  first language isn't English are exactly who this is for. Meet them warmly.
- **Helpful.** Prefer "here's how" over "you're wrong." Explain, don't gatekeep.
- **Respectful.** Disagree about ideas, never about people. No harassment, demeaning
  comments, or discrimination of any kind.
- **Focused on kids' safety.** This project handles a little bit of children's data.
  Take that seriously and never encourage collecting more than a team needs.

## Not okay

Harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, sexualized or violent content, doxxing,
or persistent unwelcome behavior. Spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any related channel.

## If something's wrong

Email **jaradd@gmail.com** or open a confidential report via GitHub. Reports are
handled discreetly and in good faith. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content,
and may warn or ban anyone who makes this space unwelcoming.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
